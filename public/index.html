More Than Just a Pizzeria.

Located in the heart of Rotterdam at Piekstraat 45, Pi! Skateria merges authentic Italian culinary tradition with urban culture. Featuring an imported wood-fired oven and chef, we serve high-quality pizzas (€10–€20) while you enjoy live skateboard entertainment.
